Icons used: mouse-pointer, pen-tool, circle, square, hexagon, star, type, image, layers-2, align-horizontal-justify-center, sliders-horizontal, eye, eye-off, shapes, undo-2, redo-2, square-function.

Modifications: all icons use a 1.5-unit rather than 2-unit stroke. The selection pointer is filled; the direct-selection pointer has an added anchor square and Canvas fill; the vertical text variant scales the type icon and adds a direction arrow. The six-color wheel is original Nect geometry, not a Lucide icon. Circle, square, and type are among the Feather-derived icons covered by the MIT section of the included license.

The complete upstream ISC and MIT license text is included as lucide-LICENSE.txt in this directory.
